# Aelo transactional email scope — 26 September 2026

Verified accounts need three lifecycle messages: a welcome message after first account provisioning, a first-results message after the onboarding measurement is persisted, and the existing evidence-qualified weekly/alert messages when enabled. Verification and password-reset messages remain mandatory and separate. Do not send a second welcome on login or imported-account claim. Do not send a first-results message for an all-failed or incomplete packet. A partial packet must be labelled partial, with no invented score or improvement. Every lifecycle delivery must be deduplicated, membership-checked, and visible as sent/failed/skipped in the existing delivery ledger. Weekly/alert preferences must not suppress account and first-results messages. Sending requires a verified sender and Resend key on the target Convex deployment; neither is checked into the repository. No production sends, billing sends, or marketing campaigns are authorized by this scope.

The email link must use the backend's configured site origin, not a hardcoded production origin. If that setting is absent or invalid, delivery should fail visibly rather than send a misleading link. Payment-provider receipts and broader campaign email need a separate verified setup and are not claimed as implemented here.
