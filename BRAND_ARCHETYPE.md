# Aelo — Brand Archetype
_Derived from concept note, strategy (PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md), competitive positioning (COMPETITORS.md), and the existing "True Noir" aesthetic. Written to be the constitution the design system, voice, and logo hang from._

---

## The recommendation, up front
**Primary: SAGE. Shading: MAGICIAN. Anti-archetypes: Hero, Jester, Innocent, Everyman.**

Everything below shows the work.

---

## What Aelo actually does for the user

The strategy doc reduces to one loop:

> **SCAN → DIAGNOSE → PRESCRIBE → ACT → RE-SCAN → PROVE**

Reframed as promises:
1. **See clearly** what the AI actually says about you (removing opacity)
2. **Understand why** it says it (attribution to sources)
3. **Act precisely** on what will move the needle (prescribed intervention)
4. **Prove impact** with a real before/after receipt (measured delta)

Two verbs dominate: **reveal** and **transform**. That's the archetype signal.

## Why Sage + Magician (and not either alone)

- **Pure Sage** ends up feeling like an academic dashboard — accurate but inert. Peec.ai lives here.
- **Pure Magician** feels like a mystical marketing tool — big promises, no rigor. Some AEO agencies live here.
- **Sage-with-Magician-shading** gets the credibility of the analyst *plus* the outcome-oriented punch of the transformer. Bloomberg + a hint of Palantir. Google + a hint of Adobe. This is our lane.

## Anti-brand (what we are explicitly NOT)

- **Not the hero.** The customer is. We never say "we help brands win" — we say "here's what will win, and here's the receipt."
- **Not fun.** Cute copy, illustrations, mascots — all wrong.
- **Not warm.** Empathy is not our register. Rigor is.
- **Not aspirational-vague.** "Unlock potential" is a Ruler/Explorer trap. We say "visibility rose 18 pts."

|---|---|---|

| Attitude | Confident, not brash | Loud, showy |

| Ambition | Standard-setting | Trend-following |

## Voice rules

1. **Data speaks; copy stays out of the way.** "Visibility +18 pts on ChatGPT" beats "Huge win! Your visibility skyrocketed."
2. **Short, declarative.** No hedging, no cute conjunctions.
3. **Prefer "we measured" / "we found"** over "we think" / "we believe."
4. **Never wink.** No emojis in product copy. No "🚀." One exception: the receipt (Magician moment) can be quietly celebratory: "You did X. Answer moved."
5. **Numbers with units.** Always.
6. **Names of things are lowercase-monospace when technical.** `chatgpt`, `gemini`, `citations`.

## Design system implications (the mandate the visual work follows)

| Dimension | Sage direction | Magician shading |
|---|---|---|
| **Color** | Pure black base, restrained monochrome, single high-signal accent | The accent shifts subtly *when a receipt is shown* — the one moment of magic |
| **Typography** | Geometric sans (Inter/System) as primary + monospace (JetBrains) for data. Big headline sizes for authority. | Tabular numerals throughout. Numbers are the story. |
| **Grid** | Dense, structured, information-per-inch high. Bloomberg-terminal-ish. | Interventions/receipts get a slightly airier treatment — signal the pivot from observation → transformation |
| **Motion** | Precise, purposeful. No bouncy easings. | Only the *reveal of a measured delta* animates — the number counts up. Everywhere else, motion is snap. |
| **Iconography** | Single-weight, thin-stroke, geometric. Feels like measurement instruments. | — |
| **Data viz** | Chart chrome minimized; ink-to-data ratio maximized (Tufte). | Delta indicators get color (up = accent, down = data-red). |

## Logo direction

The current mark (chevron/triangle silhouette) is Sage-compatible — reads as apex, prism, standard. Direction: **keep the geometric mark, sharpen its reading as a "prism/refraction" symbol.** A prism takes an invisible beam (AI answer) and reveals its spectrum (visibility, sentiment, position, citations, sources). That's Aelo in one glyph.

The name **Aelo** also plays into this — echoes *halo* (visibility, revelation), *aer* (Greek: air, atmosphere), *Aeolus* (invisible force made visible). Sage-Magician harmony baked in.

**Wordmark:** custom-tuned Inter or similar — flat sides, restrained tracking, no ligatures. Lowercase feels more Sage than uppercase (Bloomberg is uppercase-Ruler; Google/Palantir lean lowercase-Sage).
